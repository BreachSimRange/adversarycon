What to Expect

The Pre-Con Meetup is designed for offensive cyber security professionals, red teamers, threat intelligence analysts, and cyber security researchers who want to connect in a more intimate setting before the main conference begins. This exclusive event offers a unique opportunity to network with speakers, sponsors, and fellow attendees in a relaxed atmosphere.

The evening will feature lightning talks from industry experts, live demonstrations of cutting-edge offensive techniques, and plenty of time for meaningful conversations. Whether you're a seasoned professional or an emerging talent in the field, this is your chance to build connections that could shape your career.

Guest Speaker

Dr. Gregory Carpenter

Chief Security Officer

KnowledgeBridge International Inc.

Gregory Carpenter is the CSO of KnowledgeBridge International, a Fellow of the Royal Society for the Arts, and the National Security Agency’s Operations Officer of the Year. He serves on the Board of Directors for ATNA Systems, is a Senior Advisor for ARIC, Inc., and is a Special Operations Medical Association and Military Cyber Professionals Association member.
His background includes work at the NSA and three decades in government, he has led numerous operations combatting cybercrime, adversarial activity, and counterexploitation theory. He has held various senior military and civilian positions, including COO, VP for Cyber Operations, Chief of Security Testing, Counterintelligence Division Chief, Chief of Special Space Operations, and Functional Team Lead for Electronic Warfare. He has been an epidemiological primary investigator.
